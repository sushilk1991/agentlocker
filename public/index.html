Extra approval rules
Agents always need approval to delete, replace, or send. These gates cannot be removed. Extra rules can also require review for reads, new writes, folder listings, or secret listings. Rules do not grant access.


Saving rules invalidates pending and ready requests. Already dispatched sends cannot be recalled.
